# kOSMos: agent notes

Follows the portfolio standard: https://github.com/jibes/apps-data/blob/main/STANDARD.md
List deviations from it here, each with a reason.

## Stack

- TypeScript strict, React 19, Vite, MapLibre GL JS 6, vite-plugin-pwa, Vitest, Playwright, Capacitor 8 (Android). Node: see `.nvmrc`.
- Check library APIs against the installed types in `node_modules`, not from memory. MapLibre 6 has no default export: `import * as maplibregl`.

## Product rules (MECE)

Five jobs, one control each: **view** (the map), **find** (search), **locate** (location button), **inspect** (tap a label / long-press → place card with share + route; the route card is part of inspect), **go** (Start on the route card → turn-by-turn). A new feature must fit one of them or argue for a sixth. No settings screen: theme follows the system (while navigating it follows the sun instead: light by day, dark after sunset and in tunnels), language follows the browser.

## Architecture

- `src/core/` is pure logic with no DOM, and every module has tests in `tests/`. The whole map look is `core/palette.ts` (colours) + `core/style.ts` (layers, OpenMapTiles schema). Colours never appear in the style directly; a test enforces it.
- `src/ui/` holds components and map glue (`useMap`, markers, gestures) and may import from `core/`, never the other way round.
- Map labels use self-hosted Inter through the style's `font-faces`, registered under the glyph server's font names (`FONT` in `core/style.ts`) so other scripts fall back to the same weight.
- The map's own places come in by importance as it zooms in (`POI_TIERS` in `core/style.ts`, OpenMapTiles' `rank`, 1 the most important in its patch of map: up to 3 at zoom 13, 14 from 14, 30 from 15, every one from 16; a layer per tier, all in `POI_LAYERS`, tapped alike), their spacing tightening closer in (`text-padding` 6 → 2 px). Each shows its kind as an icon on its point, the name below (`core/stylePoi.ts`: the `poi` subclass, or a shop's class, to `PlaceKind` as the search rows have it (`POI_SUBCLASSES` in `core/placeKind.ts`), a disc tinted by the kind's group, `POI_GROUP`, `palette.poi*`; a place of no known kind its name alone); `ui/poiIcons.ts` draws each disc with the kind's line drawing cut out of it, one SDF image per kind registered as the map is made (the same path data as `KindIcon`, `glyphOf` in `ui/icons.tsx`).
- MapLibre's worker is bundled with `?url` and set via `setWorkerUrl` (`ui/useMap.ts`); without it the worker 404s after bundling.
- Compass: `core/compass.ts` (pure: heading from orientation events, tilt-aware: the top of the screen lying flat, the back held upright, since alpha alone is up to 90° off upright; `filterHeading` smooths by time, not per reading (phones send 10–60 a second): heavily damped held still, quick through a real turn, a one-euro filter on the circle; course vs compass by speed) and `ui/useCompass.ts` (streams headings to subscribers, not React state; phones read magnetic north, so each reading adds the declination at the last fix, `core/magnetic.ts`: WMM2025, pure, tested against NOAA's values; the GPS course is true north already; Chromium's own `deviceorientationabsolute` alone where it has one, as its `deviceorientation` is a second, relative sensor; `need` detaches the listeners while nothing shows a heading, `ui/useDotInView.ts`: the dot off the view, not turning the map with it, not guiding; the filter kept for when it comes back). The map has two buttons, one job each (`ui/Controls.tsx`): the location button says what the camera tracks (`Follow`; without a route a toggle, `aria-pressed`, following already it only flies back when the map shows elsewhere; with a route open or guided it names its next step, no `aria-pressed`: the user, and following the whole route, `route`), the needle says which way is up (heading-up `compass` ⇄ north up, and north and flat after a hand's turn or tilt; shown while following or turned).
- Category search: `core/categories.ts` (pure: the kinds of place are `core/categoryTable.ts`, each with its OpenMapTiles `poi` subclasses and hand-written words in en/de; iD's preset words per subclass are generated into `core/placeWords.ts` by `npm run words`, rerun after changing a kind's values (a test checks); a subclass in a group and a kind of its own gives its preset words to the own kind, and a word one kind names by hand stays its; `fold` ignores case, accents and ß/ss; stations, tram and bus stops are kinds too, and `stops` all of them at once (Haltestelle, ÖPNV), and their rows and dots carry `stop` so their cards show departures; `categoriesFor` suggests at most two, the word as typed before its plural, then beginnings; nearest first) and `ui/categorySearch.ts`, which fetches and decodes the zoom-14 tiles of the view itself (`core/tiles.ts`; only zoom 14 holds every place, the map's own lower-zoom tiles keep only important ones; at most 48 tiles, nearest the centre, each read once, across the 180th meridian too (the centre taken into the bounds' frame); at most `SHOWN_MAX` (60) places of a kind, the nearest, "60+" in the sheet; a new category from a view wider than zoom 12 zooms in once; searching an area again never moves the map; with none in view, the tiles under the view's edges, then up to 64 more around it, 16 at a time nearest first until some hold one: the 5 nearest, `beyond` and said so above them); no search server. Suggestions are rows above the Photon names, taken by tap or Enter; choosing one closes the list (the input blurs) for `ui/ResultsSheet.tsx`, the places by distance (from `useCategory().results`), a strip at first on phones, open in the side layout, kept hidden under a place card so it comes back as it was, the place opened last (from a row or a dot) marked; a row held, hovered or focused lights its dot (`highlight`: feature-state `hot` on the `search` source, ids in the list's order, reset with new results); editing the text leaves the category. Enter on several names without a row chosen (arrows or hover set one) shows them all as a `found:<text>` kind: Photon's places handed to the map, nearest first, the view fitted to the best and those within 30 km (`nearBest`); one name opens it. Every place row (names, results, recent places) starts with an icon for what it is (`core/placeKind.ts`, pure: Photon's OSM key and value or the tiles' `poi` subclass → train, tram, bus, café, shop, town, street, address… else a pin; kept with a recent place). Results are dots and names in the style's `search` source, above labels and tappable (a dot opens its result as its row does, `searchResultAt`, no lookup by name); `ui/useCategory.ts` owns them, "show all" (Enter) and the "Search this area" offer after the user's own pan or zoom (40 % of a view or a zoom step; told by how a move ends: a gesture's own event, or `ASKED` as event data on the location button's flight; the app's other moves, following the position or show all, re-anchor the results unless the offer is already up; with the offer up the search key searches here, as the offer does; a fit to results (`onFit`) stops following, and the location button, while following but the map elsewhere, first flies back to the user (`offCentre`)); they hide while a route is open. Nameless toilets, ATMs, car parks, playgrounds, post boxes, drinking water, recycling, charging points and taxi ranks count (`UNNAMED`, called by their kind). Distances count from the user when in view, else from the middle of the map beside the panel (the side layout's map starts at the panel); each tile read gives up after 8 s. An empty field (`core/searchList.ts`, pure: the rows of the list, `Item` by `type`; `ui/SearchRow.tsx` draws one) has the lists as pills in one strip (`r-strip`, a `group` of options; icon, name, how many; the kinds as "Categories"), apart from the recent searches below under their heading (`r-heading`), each opening in the field's place (`Folder`, kept in `useSearch` beside the text; going in or out empties the text; the list slides, `data-slide`): favourites (how many), contacts (how many placed; none yet: the row that adds them), the kinds of place (every kind, `COMMON` first, then by name); in a list its first row goes back (Escape, Back too, `App.tsx`'s ladder), the text filters it on the device only (a name's beginning or a word's; favourites by the user's label or the place's name), and places come nearest first with how far (`byDistance`, from `distanceFrom` in `ui/categorySearch.ts`: the user in view, else the map's middle beside the panel); the contacts' own row (add, how many, the cross) is in theirs. Below the lists the recent searches (`core/recent.ts`, pure: 8, 30 days, one per place or kind; `ui/useRecent.ts` keeps them in `kosmos.recent.v1`, shared by both fields, the route's own taking places only; names chosen from Photon's, and a place from the map once something is done with it on its card: Route, Share, the star or Nearby (`rememberPlace`, `recentPlaceOf`: a dropped pin by its address, never a bare point or the user's position; a card only opened is not kept, or every tap would push out the searches); a favourite opened enters them, so a frequent one stays one tap; a stop or station stays one, `stop`); nothing is preselected, Delete or the cross forgets one. The place card's Nearby centres the map there, opens the kinds of place and focuses the search (in the tap, so a phone opens its keyboard); distances then count from the middle of the map, as whenever the user is not in view. The search's text and category live in App (`useSearch` in `ui/SearchBar.tsx`), so they outlive a route; choosing a route's start or destination is its own name-only bar. More ways to say where (`core/queryForms.ts`, pure): coordinates (decimal, or degrees, minutes and seconds with hemisphere letters, German O for east: `degreesIn` in `core/shared.ts`) and map links typed or pasted are the point itself (`parseShared`; an openstreetmap.org object link opens the object through `openShared` in `ui/useShared.ts`, a message's link is left out of the words searched), as is a Plus Code (`plusCode`: full codes decoded on the device, a short one near the map's middle, or after a place's name near that place, found first); nothing found for a text of 4 or more as typed, at most two other forms are asked one by one (`otherForms`: a postcode written properly and then the area it names, `postcodeOf`: Eircode's routing key, the UK's outward code, Canada's, the Netherlands' digits, else the first part of one written with a space or dash, both parts with a digit ("12 Main" is none); the house number on the other side; without the last word), said above the names (`foundInstead`). Photon slower than 5 s has Nominatim asked meanwhile, and its own answer still taken whenever it comes (given up at 20 s); text left in a closed or hidden field is never asked again for a new area (`areaThen`, the area as of when the list was last open). Photon is asked for 15 names (the list scrolls; a finger pulling it down from its top, 64 px or a flick, blurs the field: list and keyboard away, the text kept, back on focus); a query that is only the word for a kind the tiles do not list (airports: Flughafen, airport…) asks for that OSM tag alone (`worldKind` in `core/search.ts`). Map taps are `ui/useMapTaps.ts`: a label's details come from the nearest Photon hit of its name within a radius by kind of label (150 m for places), else a reverse lookup of its spot's address and no OSM id.
- Opening hours: `core/hours.ts` (pure: the common `opening_hours` subset; anything else is shown as written, never guessed: holidays or closing days alone, too) and `ui/PlaceDetails.tsx`; open or closed now only for places on about the device's clock (`nearDeviceClock`, round the clock: Samoa at 172° W is UTC+13), elsewhere just the week. Route boxes over the 180th meridian are the narrow box over it (`boundsOfLine` in `core/geo.ts`).
- Lanes: `core/lanes.ts` (pure: Valhalla `turn_lanes` per maneuver, shown only when some lanes lead the right way and some do not; size by distance and speed). Comes with the same route request.
- Navigation theme: `core/daylight.ts` (pure: sun elevation, tunnel match against `brunnel=tunnel` lines) and `ui/useNavTheme.ts`, which reads tunnels from the tiles already loaded every 4 s, new tiles within a second (never on every tile, which arrive all the time while driving) (Valhalla's native response has no tunnel flag); dark holds in a tunnel until a fix outside (GPS often stops there), and each drive starts undecided; at night or out of sight no tunnel is read, and the tile listener goes with guidance. While navigating it decides both ways (`data-theme` on `<html>`): the system's dark media query yields to `data-theme='light'`, and `styles/base.css` repeats the dark tokens under `data-theme='dark'`.
- Tickets: the transit route card lists each operator of the connection's rides once, in the order ridden, as a link to its own website (`ticketsOf` in `core/transit.ts`, MOTIS's `agencyName`/`agencyUrl`, from GTFS `agency`; only http(s) addresses; `Tickets` in `ui/TransitParts.tsx`, opened in the browser). No booking with the journey filled in: no timetable data names one, and operators' own deep links are undocumented and change.
- Public transport guidance is `core/ride.ts` (pure, replay-tested like `nav.ts`): walk → wait → ride → arrived from fixes and time; GPS when a fix is fresh, the timetable's arrival times underground. `ui/useRide.ts` also ticks every 3 s so it moves on without fixes; `ui/RideView.tsx` renders it; its banner opens the whole trip (`TransitLegs`, every leg closed at first; a ride's times in one column with boarding and getting off on its progress line, passed stops dimmed). A missed departure (still walking once the vehicle has left; on board only when the position moves along its line; a ride entered on the timetable alone, without a fix, is provisional until the next fix) or a cancelled trip offers a new connection (one request, user-initiated, from the stop when the fix is old; the followed connection stays until it comes); a late vehicle keeps the ride going while the position is on its line. Still standing at the boarding stop 3 min after the departure, not along the line, is missed too (`LEFT_BEHIND_MS`; a late vehicle that comes takes the rider along the line, which ends it). While a new connection is asked for, the one followed stays (`useRoute` keeps a pinned transit result across the key change, and on failure as `kept`). Muted, ride prompts buzz (`rideBuzz` in `core/haptics.ts`: boarding and getting off like a turn, missed, cancelled and a new platform as a warning, arrival long). Start on a plan for "now" whose first vehicle has left plans again instead (`departed`, one user-initiated request). The progress line counts only stops served (`servedBefore`); skipped ones are struck through. A ride ends at its stop (within the fix's accuracy, at least 40 m), never at the one before; arrival is said only on a position at the destination, never on the timetable alone. What changes is said once each: missed, cancelled, a new platform, a delay of 3 min or more.
- Stops on the way: `core/stops.ts` (pure: at most `MAX_STOPS` (3), by road only; the router is asked by way of each, Valhalla `break` locations, no alternates; `toRoute` marks each leg's last maneuver but the last's as `stop`, `nameStops` says it by name, "Stop at …", and learns its place in `Trip.via` as `via`; `stopsReached` counts those behind, at one within `ARRIVE_M` or past it, never off the route) and `ui/useStops.ts` (added from the route card's "Add stop" (its own name-only picker), a point held on the map or, with a route open and also while guiding, a label tapped). A route's points are one list, all alike (`core/points.ts`: `pointsOf`, `endsOf` back into `from`/`via`/`to`, "my location" anywhere but first the position now and back at the start live again; `movePoint`, `removePoint` (two at least), `addPoint` before the end, `MAX_POINTS`; `ui/PointList.tsx`: a grip dragged by pointer (`data-no-swipe`, `touch-action: none`, so the sheet's own swipe and scrolling leave it alone) or moved by the arrow keys with its focus kept, dropped rows taking their places at once (`data-settling`, no slide from the old), the route card keeping the last ways found, dimmed and Start disabled, while a changed trip is asked for, so nothing below the list jumps, the name a button for another place by name (`picking` is the point's index, or `'add'`), a cross while more than two); the route card shows them all (public transport its two ends, its stops kept for the road), and guiding the banner's panel the ones ahead after "my location", locked (`locked`, `numberFrom` so stops keep the map's numbers), each change one route from here, the way followed kept until it comes (`waiting` in `useRoute`: both keys from here to the same end, `sameEnd`; a new destination waits instead, as the old way would arrive, and end the trip, at the old one; stops are counted only on a current route, `current`, as a kept one numbers them by its own list). No swap button: dragging the end to the top is the swap; numbered `stop-dot` markers in `useRouteLayer`; with a route open the destination's pin is the trip's end, whatever the list now ends with (`end` in `App.tsx`, not the selected place, which follows it quietly so closing the route shows the destination's card; `select` would stop following while guiding); a stop held on the map takes its address like a held start or end (`spotOf`), its toast's Undo matching it by its point; while guiding the stops reached go to `useRoute` (`stopsBehind`, read at request time, not part of the key), so a reroute goes on to the rest; a stop added while guiding asks one route from here, the followed one kept should it fail; a new stop goes in its order along the shown way (`stopIndex`), not last; its toast undoes it; while guiding only the stops ahead count against `MAX_STOPS`; `behind` is reset at Start, before the route from here is asked). A label tapped with a route open, also while guiding by road, offers Go here as well: the new destination, one route from here. The swap reverses them too (`swapEnds`). A stop left behind while guiding (off the route and `SKIP_AWAY_M` further from it than the closest yet: car 1 km, bike 400 m, foot 150 m; `leavingStop`) is asked about aloud and in a note, Skip or Keep, and skipped by itself after `SKIP_DELAY_MS` (10 s; `ui/useStopSkip.ts`): skipped counts as reached and asks one route from here; kept is not asked again that drive; muted, it buzzes the warning.
- Along the way: `core/alongRoute.ts` (pure: the rest of a road route within `ALONG[mode]`'s band either side and reach ahead, car 500 m / 50 km, bike 250 m / 15 km, foot 150 m / 3 km; `tilesAlong` the zoom-14 tiles under the band in the order the way reaches them, at most `ALONG_TILES` (60); `placesAlong` nearest along the way first, at most `ALONG_MAX` (30), each with its metres ahead and a detour estimated from its distance off the route there and back (no router asked; under `ON_THE_WAY_M` it is on the way); `TRIP_KINDS` the kinds offered) and `ui/AlongRoute.tsx` (chips of those kinds and a list of every other (`Other…`), the list, the places as dots in the style's `along` source, tappable like labels (Add stop, Go here), Add stop through `useStops`' `addStopRef`; `findAlong` in `ui/categorySearch.ts` reads the tiles with the category search's own cache; `ui/useAlongSearch.ts` wires it): on the route card before setting off ("Along the way" beside "Add stop", its panel under them, closed again for another way of travel), and while guiding in the panel of the points ahead, its distances counting on as the trip goes (from `nav.snap.along`), places passed dropping off.
- Fits keep room: `core/fitRoom.ts` (`within`: a card grown tall leaves `MIN_FREE_PX` (120) of map, what gives coming off its own side first, never nearer the edge than `EDGE_PX` (48)); `routePadding` caps the card's side at the screen less the top's room and that.
- Route drawing, the fit (`fit`, also the location button's next step while following with the route card open) and the start marker are `ui/useRouteLayer.ts`, so `App.tsx` stays the wiring. The route card's swap button exchanges its ends (`onSwap`); "My location" as a destination is the fix then, marked `Place.here`, and swapped back becomes `from: null`, the live position, again.
- Guidance views: `core/navView.ts` (pure: pitch and zoom per way of travel, car 55°, bike 30°, foot from above; the zoom by speed a line, never steps (`between` over `CAR_ZOOM`, `BIKE_ZOOM`), and closer as a turn comes, up to 0.8 levels by car and 0.5 by bike over the last 12 s of it or 150 m (`navZoomFor`'s `toTurn`); what is left of a route or trip) and `ui/useNavView.ts` (`overview`, and the user's own zoom and tilt, `Adjust`, kept for the trip). The controls are the map's own (`Controls`, MECE: what the camera tracks is nothing, the user or the rest of the way; following, which way is up is as turned by hand or heading-up): the location button follows as on the map (off → the mode left, heading-up as each trip starts), the needle switches heading-up and north up (road guidance turns heading-up by the way it goes, public transport by the compass; north up is flat, heading-up the way of travel's own tilt); following, the location button's next step is the overview, and from it the user again (`WholeRouteIcon`: start, a dashed way, the end; `ui/useNavControls.ts`). A drag stops following and leaves the overview; a pinch or tilt never stops following (read off the map at the next fix, `lookedRef`), a twist leaves heading-up for following as turned; in the overview any gesture lets go. The overview is `fitNav` (the rest of the way, north up: flat first, then the fit, as zooming out while tilted shows far past the route and seems to zoom back in; on phones the sides keep 25 % of the width, the map buttons' side at least their column and 24 px). Between fixes the arrow runs on (`core/glide.ts`, pure: dead reckoning at the speed measured, along the road itself on the route (`pointAt` in `core/nav.ts`), along its course off it, at most `AHEAD_S` (2 s) past the last fix, pulled toward where that fix puts it by now (`GLIDE_TAU_S`), never back down the road while moving, its heading turning smoothly; a fix only corrects its pace, so late, doubled or bunched fixes (Android's) never make it stop and jump; a fix with the same time as the last is skipped; `ui/glide.ts` is one loop of frames while anything moves, the camera's turn, zoom and tilt easing from wherever the map is toward what the last fix asked for, within the map's limits; a frame that would move nothing by a quarter pixel draws nothing, as each draw is a full render), the follow camera with it frame by frame (`jumpTo`, `followCamera` in `layout.ts`), so the arrow keeps its place on screen and never jumps; a finger, a pinch read later, the overview or a button's own move (`release`) leave the camera alone, the arrow gliding on. Each fix's dot and camera outside road guidance are `ui/useFixCamera.ts` (no move for a fix within 2 px of the middle, `nearCentre`: GPS jitter standing still would otherwise ease the map, rendering, every second). Public transport's camera is `ui/useRideCamera.ts`: the dot while walking or waiting (turned as the button says), on board the rest of the line to the stop to get off, the overview the rest of the trip. The ride's opened stops are a progress line (`data-passed`, `data-next`). Map gestures and the user's dot are `ui/useMapGestures.ts` (a first finger, or the app put away, clears fingers whose "up" never came, so the camera never waits for good).
- Where a way leads: `core/roundabout.ts` (pure, `narrateWays`) prefers the signs over a road's name or number, which run both ways. Any maneuver but leaving, arriving and coming off a roundabout with a signed destination (`Maneuver.toward`, Valhalla's `sign.exit_toward_elements`) gets it as `wayOut` ("towards Tullamore"), added to its instruction, alert and pre prompts unless the router said it already; a roundabout also takes its exit's signs. Only a sign names a place: guessing the next town on the route named places no sign there shows (`core/towns.ts` is gone); without one the road's name and number stand, and a roundabout without a sign the direction the exit road leads 120 m on (`headingFrom`, 8 compass points). The banner shows `wayOut` big and the street small below (`short`, `road` in `ui/NavView.tsx`); a roundabout as "2nd exit · towards Tullamore" (`ordinal` in `i18n`). Without signs it stays the street. A motorway junction's number (`exit_number_elements`, `Maneuver.junction`) leads the banner ("Exit 14a · towards Galway") and is said unless the router's words have it (`withJunction`). Route numbers on the signs (`is_route_number`: `destination:ref` in `exit_branch_elements`, `destination:ref:to` in `exit_toward_elements`) are `Maneuver.refs`, kept out of `toward`; `roadOf` shows the road as signed: the sign's number over the road's own (Ireland's downgraded N roads: OSM says R, the sign N), a name with the number after it; a roundabout takes its exit's. Route fits keep 56 px round both ends for their markers (`ENDS_PX`), the overview measured from the banner and the bar.
- The navigation arrow (`me-arrow` in `ui/markers.ts`, `styles/navigation.css`): an SVG outlined in white, darker than the route's blue so it never melts into it, facing the screen while navigating (`pitchAlignment: 'viewport'`), as lying on the tilted map squashes it.
- Speed limits: `core/speedLimit.ts` (pure: OSM `maxspeed` from the router's `trace_attributes`, `edge.speed_limit` as stretches by distance along the route, scaled to its length; "unlimited" shows no sign; red when over by more than 5 % and at least 3 km/h, `overLimit`; mph where roads count in miles) and `ui/useRouteTrace.ts` (one POST per route guided by road, a reroute brings another; none for the route card); `NavView` shows the sign on the speed, off the route none.
- Junctions as they lie: `core/junction.ts` (pure: the same `trace_attributes` asks for each node's `intersecting_edges` with their `begin_heading` and whether the way of travel can use them; `junctionsOf` finds the node at each maneuver (within 15 m) and gives a turn as the way out and the other roads there (across a dual carriageway the roads at both ends of the router's `internal_intersection` edge, one junction; a way out within 30° of straight back, a U-turn, keeps its arrow), a roundabout as the exit taken, the exits passed (roads leaving the ring before it) and which way round (`drive_on_right`), all in degrees from the way in; `junctionPaths` draws them in 24 units, the way in from the bottom, the way taken bold with its arrowhead, the rest faint) and `JunctionIcon` in `ui/icons.tsx`: the banner's big icon wherever the trace knows the junction, else the arrow for its kind; the step after keeps its small arrow.
- Heading while navigating: `NavState.moving` (hysteresis 3 → 1.5 m/s; progress counts only beyond GPS jitter, max(6 m, accuracy/2)) picks the route's course over the compass; standing still never flips it. On the route by car or bike the route's direction holds standing too (traffic, a light: a mounted phone is no compass; `held` in `travelHeading`); on foot, and off the route, standing turns it by the compass.
- Steps ahead: the banner always shows the step after the next (`then`, its distance from here beyond 150 m, the last one by the destination's name); its toggle ("Show stops ahead") opens the points ahead in `ui/TripDetails.tsx` (also while off the route; on a phone the map buttons step aside meanwhile) (the same swipe panel as the ride's trip list, `tripList` in `App.tsx`, Back closes it). No list of every turn: the overview shows the way better.
- Guidance UI: `ui/NavBar.tsx` (time left, mute, End with a second tap; the voice is `useMuted` in `ui/voice.ts`: one choice kept for the next trip in `kosmos.muted.v1`, on at first; muted on foot or by bike, prompts buzz instead (`core/haptics.ts`: the alert once, the turn twice, arrival long; `vibrate` in `ui/native.ts`, `KosmosPlugin.vibrate` on Android, the browser's own elsewhere); arrival ends guidance and the trip at once, `t.arrived` as a toast over the destination's card; the voice is not stopped then, so the arrival prompt is said whole). `NavWaiting` (in `ui/NavView.tsx`) stands in whenever guidance has no route yet, so End is always on screen. Off the route the arrow and camera follow the fix itself; the banner says "Off the route", "Finding a new route…" only while one is asked. The needle's north up is held until the map is there (`aimRef` in `useGuidance`), as a fix mid-ease would stop it part-way. The app's own camera moves wait while a finger is on the map (`touching` in `App.tsx`): any move resets MapLibre's gesture handlers. Long-press does nothing while guiding. A failed reroute keeps the old route (`useRoute` reports `kept`), and the banner says so only while still off the route.
- Guidance is `core/nav.ts`: pure, fed fixes and time, covered by replay tests. Fixes worse than `COARSE_M` (65 m: network, tunnels, approximate) move the dot only (`useGuidance` places it at the fix; arrow, camera and guidance wait); a turn is done only 10 m past it on the road after it (going straight on, or waiting at the light, takes no turn); off the route `prompt` stays quiet; off the line is 35–80 m by the fix's accuracy (`offRouteLimit`), on foot from 20 m (`Track.mode`: a parallel path or the far side of a square), for 8 s; the same for every road mode, and reroutes ask with the trip's mode; going back down the route (40 m, or a course against it 3 fixes in a row) is off it; getting to the route from a car park is not. `ui/useGuidance.ts` wires it to the map: the arrow, the camera (both the user and the way back when off the route), rerouting that keeps the followed route until a new one arrives; the last fix counts at once at Start, and with the first fix come the route's first words (`0:pre`), or, more than `HEAD_M` (30 m) from where it starts, "Head to the route" first (`0:head`, `headingToRoute`; the banner then says that and how far, nothing of what comes after; setting off never buzzes), and the dot follows while the route is still coming. Prompts are queued, never cut off (`ui/voice.ts`). A fix may only move along the route as far as plausible travel since the last one (`reachOf`), so self-crossing routes and U-turns onto the other carriageway skip nothing; arrival is 40 m along the route short of the end (`ARRIVE_M`: doors off the road, parking, GPS between buildings), said only on arriving and then latched; the early alert moves out with speed (30 s ahead). The UI only subscribes it to location (`useLocation().subscribe`) and renders `NavState`.
- Battery: nothing is drawn or sensed that is not seen. The dot's pulse stops off the view (`.away`, `useDotInView`), its rotation and accuracy ring are written only when they change visibly (`markers.ts`: 0.3°, whole pixels), the guidance loop skips sub-pixel frames, the compass rests (above, and while the app is out of sight: `isAway`; the ride camera and compass turning wait then too), and `NavLocationService` asks the network only while GPS has had no fix for `GPS_FRESH_MS` (10 s) or only approximate location is allowed. Hidden, the WebView stops rAF, geolocation and sensors itself; an e2e test (`battery.spec.ts`) covers the web side.
- Pins: `.pin`'s box ends at its tip (`styles/map.css`, anchor bottom), so it stands on the place at any zoom; an e2e test measures it.
- Sheets: `ui/Sheet.tsx` (place, route and results cards) minimise to a strip; a finger drags them from anywhere (`ui/useSwipe.ts`: 8 px, mostly vertical; a list under the finger scrolls until at its end; text fields and sideways moves are left alone; taps stay taps), a mouse from the grab bar. The ride's trip list folds away on a pull up the same way. The map buttons' column lets taps through between its buttons.

The empty search field has their list (above); typing finds them by either name (`matchFavourites`, at most 3) above contacts and Photon's names, a place Photon found too listed once.
- Marks too close to tell apart (favourites, contacts) are one group: their sources cluster (`MARK_CLUSTER` in `core/styleMarks.ts`: 36 px, up to zoom 15), a disc in their colour with how many (`*-groups`, `*-counts`), the single marks filtered to `ALONE`; a tap on a group zooms in until it parts (`zoomIntoGroup` in `ui/useMapTaps.ts`). Search results are not grouped: their highlight goes by feature id and at most 60 show.

The route's own fields find them by name (`people`), without their list. Refused in the app, a note says so with a Settings button (`importContacts` → `'denied'`); placed once, the address book is read again, never asking, on opening and on coming back after `REREAD_AFTER_MS` (10 min, `rereadDue`).

- Layout: `ui/layout.ts` (the side layout for wide screens and phones held sideways, same query as `styles.css`; camera paddings; the column's width is `--side-w` in CSS and `sideWidth()` in JS, keep them equal), `ui/usePanel.ts` (publishes the panel height as `--panel-h` so the map buttons sit just above it; keeps a selected pin clear of its card; starts location on opening only when already granted), `ui/prefs.ts` (remembered choices), `ui/motion.ts` (camera moves honour reduced motion).
- Walking and cycling routes: `core/route.ts` sends Valhalla `walkway_factor` 0.6 and `sidewalk_factor` 0.8 (`FOOT_OPTIONS`): its defaults cost a four-lane road like a footpath. Bike routes send `use_roads` 0.25 (`BIKE_OPTIONS`, Valhalla's default 0.5): cycleways and quiet streets over busy roads. By car, when every way found has toll roads, one more request with `exclude_tolls` (`needsTollFree`, `withTollFree`) adds a way without, so there is always one to choose; Valhalla's `has_toll` (`Route.toll`) marks a way with coins on its tile and "Tolls" in the summary. Starting a toll-free way chosen over toll ones keeps its reroutes toll-free (`tollFree` in `App.tsx`, read by `useRoute` at request time, not part of its key). Colour tokens `--danger` and `--late` keep text contrast in both themes; targets are at least 44 px. Wherever a message says to allow location in the settings, the app offers Settings (route card, `NavWaiting`, the locate toast). A sheet's grab bar toggles on click, so screen readers work it too; copied coordinates are said in the live region (`t.coordsCopied`). A router failing while online says the server is not answering (`t.routeDown`), offline that it needs a connection. A refusal says why where the router's own `error_code` tells (`classifyError`: 170/171 no road for this way of travel near an end, `noRoad`; 150–157 too far, `tooFar`; else no route), and the usage log keeps the code (`route.fail`). "No path" (442: roads at both ends, none between, as from hotel grounds behind a private gate, which can be reached but never left, or to a pedestrian quarter's street, Temple Bar, which can be left but never reached by car) is asked once more with any public road within `END_RADIUS_M` (150 m) of each end a candidate (`publicEnds` in `routeUrl`: `radius` and `search_filter.min_road_class` residential, stops on the way left as they are; `route.retry`).

## Invariants

- Requests: the web gives up after 20 s (`WEB_TIMEOUT_MS` in `ui/http.ts`), the app after 15 s, so a hung server ends as a failure with Retry.
- Storage keys follow `kosmos.<name>.v<N>` (`kosmos.view.v1`). Never rename an existing key: bump the version and migrate.
- A contact's address goes to Photon once, only after the user asked for contacts, never its name.
- Network calls go only to OpenFreeMap, Photon, the FOSSGIS Valhalla router, Transitous and Nominatim (README → Privacy, CSP in `index.html`).
- Nominatim (`core/details.ts`, `ui/usePlaceDetails.ts`) follows the OSMF usage policy: one `lookup` by OSM id per place card opened, cached; as search fallback one `search` for a settled query only when Photon fails or exceeds 5 s, never as-you-type (a quick failure waits until the text has been left alone 5 s, or Enter; `SearchBar.tsx`); one request a second across both (`nominatimSlot` in `ui/http.ts`), which also names the app; while it is still to be asked the list says so (`pending`, `t.searchElsewhere`), not "try again".
- Transitous (`core/transit.ts`) is volunteer-run too: one plan request per trip, travel-mode or departure change (the time field settles 800 ms first); one `stoptimes` request per stop card opened (`core/departures.ts`, around the stop's point, `ui/useDepartures.ts` keeps it a minute; a stop is a tapped `poi` label of a stop class or a Photon hit with a stop tag, `Place.stop`); while guiding, at most one `trip` check per ride (its vehicle's live times, platforms and cancellations at every stop), in the last 10 min before boarding and not within 3 min of planning (the plan's own time, `Transit.planned`, not Start's) (`ui/useRide.ts`). Never polled. Requests go through `ui/http.ts`. The Android app names itself with a User-Agent via `CapacitorHttp`; browsers send their Referer. `Mode` (road: Valhalla, turn-by-turn in `core/nav.ts`) vs `TravelMode` (adds `transit`: guided by `core/ride.ts`).
- The router is a volunteer service under fair use: one request per trip or travel-mode change (by car a second, without tolls, only when every way found has them; after "no path" one more from the public roads at the ends), re-requested by an open road route only when the start moves more than 150 m (or twice the accuracy) and at most once a minute, never for public transport (`ui/useRoute.ts`), and while navigating only on a confirmed off-route, at most every 30 s and backing off to 4 min while standing in the same place (`rerouteDue` in `core/nav.ts`), for one route leaving in the direction of travel (standing in traffic, the course last driven within `COURSE_KEEP_MS`, 2 min, `courseOf` in `core/nav.ts`: without it the router may start the new route backwards); besides, one `trace_attributes` for each route guided by road (its junctions, and by car its speed limits). Never poll it.
- A new data source or bundled asset goes into `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` (linked from About).
- No user-facing string is hard-coded. Add it to `src/i18n/en.ts` and `de.ts`.
- Android app ID `io.github.jibes.kosmos` and the release key are permanent; changing either breaks updates for installed users.

## Android

- `android/` is a committed Capacitor project; `npx cap sync android` copies `dist/` in (ignored by git).
- Icons are vector adaptive icons in `res/drawable` + `res/mipmap-anydpi`; web PNGs come from `npm run icons` (set `CHROMIUM_PATH` in cloud sessions).
- The app's own native code (registered in `MainActivity`): `KosmosPlugin.java` opens the app's system settings (offered when location was refused, `openAppSettings` in `ui/native.ts`) writes log lines under the tag `kOSMos` (`nativeLog`, `forwardErrors`) reads the contacts with a postal address when asked (`contacts`, READ_CONTACTS requested then) buzzes turns (`vibrate`, accessibility usage so the system's vibration settings apply) hands a `geo:` link to the other maps apps with kOSMos left out of the chooser (`openElsewhere`, "Open in…", so it never loops back) and hands over places from other apps (`takeShared` for the one the app opened with, a retained `shared` event after; intent filters for `geo:`, openstreetmap.org links and shared text); `NavLocationPlugin.java` + `NavLocationService.java` keep location coming while guiding: a `location`-type foreground service on the platform's `LocationManager` (GPS, and the network on its own listener only while GPS has nothing fresh, each asked whether switched on or not, and asked anew when location or a provider is switched on: Android 16 leaves a request made while location was off inactive), no Google Play services; it asks for location (on native the only prompt at Start: two at once cancel each other), once per run for precise where only approximate was given (and the app explains with a Settings button), and on Android 13+ for the notification once. `useLocation().background(true)` starts the WebView watch once the plugin has its answer and keeps it until the service's first fix. `KosmosPlugin.duck` holds navigation audio focus while prompts speak (other audio ducks; a prompt stopped before ends late without lifting it under a newer one, `round` in `nativeVoice`); the service's notification shows what the banner says, readable on the lock screen (`NavLocationPlugin.update`, `ui/useNavNotice.ts`: only changes, at most once a second); the status bar's icons follow the guidance theme (`SystemBars`).
- Picture-in-picture: `MainActivity` (`supportsPictureInPicture`; `setPip` from `KosmosPlugin.pip`, called by `ui/usePip.ts` while guiding: Android 12+ enters by itself on the way home, `setAutoEnterEnabled`, earlier versions on `onUserLeaveHint`; portrait 3:4), and tells the page with a `kosmospip` window event (`triggerWindowJSEvent`, `on`); guidance ending in the small window sends the app to the back; the page sets `data-pip` on `<html>` and shows only the banner's turn and distance over the following map (`styles/navigation.css`; `navPadding` gives the banner the top), everything else hidden, as taps in the window open the app.
- Out of sight while guiding the page keeps running: `KosmosWebView` (the app's own `capacitor_bridge_layout_main.xml`) tells the page it is shown while `KosmosPlugin.keepRunning` is on (`usePip`, while guiding), as a hidden page is throttled and frozen and turns were only said once the app was opened again (the usage log showed whole stretches silent); `MainActivity` says whether it really is with `kosmosaway` (`isAway` in `ui/native.ts`; the glide draws nothing then, the log writes `app.away`/`app.back`).
- Release builds log nothing from the WebView (`loggingBehavior: 'debug'`): the bridge would otherwise write every fix to logcat. Too old a WebView (< 111, `minWebViewVersion`: the styles need `color-mix()`, `dvh`, `:has()`) shows `public/webview.html` (static, both languages, outside i18n since the app cannot run there).
- Back button: `ui/useBack.ts` (`@capacitor/app`) and Escape share one ladder in `App.tsx` (About → picker → navigation, which closes an open trip list first and then needs a second press → route → place → search); with nothing left back minimises the app instead of quitting.
- The page's renderer keeps the app's own priority (`setRendererPriorityPolicy(IMPORTANT, false)` in `MainActivity`): by default it drops when the app is not on screen and Android freezes it after a while, so guidance went silent in the background (fixes still reaching the service) and spoke every queued prompt on return. Prompts are said only about a fix under 15 s old (`sayable` in `core/nav.ts`; ride prompts too), so a queue never reads out turns long passed.
- Navigation on Android: `useLocation().background(true)` moves location to `NavLocationService`; speech uses the system engine (`ui/voice.ts`), keep-awake the native plugin (`ui/wakeLock.ts`). The web build falls back to Web Speech and Screen Wake Lock.
- CI boots an Android 16 (API 36) emulator after the build and runs `scripts/android-smoke.sh` before any release: install without any permission granted, launch, no crash, no web errors, `kosmos:ready` logged under the tag `kOSMos` once the map loads; then guidance's location service (started as root, location switched off, then on): in the foreground, and `navigation: first fix` logged once `adb emu geo fix` arrives. The build job has no secrets (npm and Gradle run there, also for pull requests) and leaves the APK unsigned, with a throwaway-signed copy for the emulator; the `release` job (main only, `release` environment) waits for the web app's own checks and end-to-end tests too (`ci.yml`, called as `web`), then first checks the version (already tagged: nothing to do; only a plain x.y.z newer than the last tag), then signs that tested APK with apksigner, checks the certificate against README and publishes. The Gradle wrapper JAR and distribution are checked against gradle.org's checksums. Its artifact `android-smoke` holds a screenshot with real tiles and the logcat; the latest screenshot is also force-pushed to the `ci-smoke` branch.
- CI (`ci.yml`, called by the deploy and by the Android release, a concurrency group per caller): the quick checks in one job, the end-to-end tests in four shards at once (`--shard`, `fullyParallel`, so tests split evenly; every test stands alone).
- Release: bump `package.json` version on main → `android.yml` publishes `v<version>` if that tag does not exist.

## Deviations

- `android.yml` uses the runner's preinstalled JDK 21 and Android SDK instead of setup actions, and boots the emulator with the SDK's own tools instead of `android-emulator-runner` (fewer third-party actions to pin).
- Fonts and tiles for scripts beyond Latin come from OpenFreeMap's glyph server at runtime; self-hosting all of Noto would add tens of MB.
